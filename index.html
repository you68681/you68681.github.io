Chao Lei

PhD Candidate in the AI and Autonomy Lab at The University of Melbourne.

I am advised by Assoc. Prof. Nir Lipovetzky and Assoc. Prof. Kris Ehinger. My research focuses on AI planning, large language models, and reasoning, with an interest in how structured planning methods can improve generalization and problem solving.

I am happy to hear from people interested in AI planning, LLM reasoning, generalized planning, and abstraction.
